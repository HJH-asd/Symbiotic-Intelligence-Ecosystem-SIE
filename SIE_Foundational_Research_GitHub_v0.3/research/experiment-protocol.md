# Comparative Experiment Protocol / 对照实验协议

## English

**Research question:** Which governance and resource-allocation mechanisms preserve cooperation and correction under scarcity and asymmetric power?

**Environment:** Fully isolated abstract multi-agent simulation; no real accounts, networks, infrastructure, or self-replicating code.

**Baselines:** (A) centralized allocator, (B) competitive allocation, (C) polycentric coordination, (D) SIE-inspired modular/exit/audit mechanisms. Match total resources and information assumptions where possible.

**Factors:** resource scarcity, shock frequency, control concentration, ability to exit, auditing independence, transition protection, and agent behavioral heterogeneity.

**Metrics:** cooperation rate; essential-service continuity; resource concentration; correction completion rate and delay; harms/violations; ability to recover; strategy diversity; coordination cost.

**Procedure:** pre-register scenarios and metrics; run multiple random seeds; report distributions and uncertainty; compare ablations; test robustness to alternative agent models; document failures and unintended effects.

**Safety:** simulation only. Never implement propagation, privilege escalation, unauthorized resource capture, or harmful real-world social experiments.

**Interpretation:** results establish properties of the model under its assumptions, not universal truths about humans or AI.

## 中文

**研究问题：** 哪些治理与资源分配机制能在匮乏和权力不对称下维持合作与纠错？

**环境：** 完全隔离的抽象多智能体仿真；不接入真实账户、网络、基础设施，也不使用自复制代码。

**基线：** A集中分配、B竞争分配、C多中心协调、D受SIE启发的模块化／退出／审计机制。尽可能控制总资源和信息条件一致。

**变量：** 资源匮乏、冲击频率、控制集中度、退出能力、审计独立性、转型保障、代理行为异质性。

**指标：** 合作率、关键服务连续性、资源集中度、纠错完成率与延迟、伤害／违规、恢复能力、策略多样性、协调成本。

**步骤：** 预注册情境与指标；采用多随机种子；报告分布和不确定性；做消融比较；检验对不同代理模型的敏感性；记录失败及副作用。

**安全：** 仅限模拟。不实现传播、提权、越权资源攫取，也不进行危险的现实社会实验。

**解释：** 结果只能支持模型假设下的结论，不能直接推导人类或AI的普遍规律。
