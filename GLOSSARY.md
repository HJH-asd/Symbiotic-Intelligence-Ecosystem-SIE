# Glossary / 术语表

| Term | English meaning | 中文说明 |
|---|---|---|
| SIE | Symbiotic Intelligence Ecosystem: a provisional cross-intelligence research framework | 共生智能生态系统：暂定的跨智能研究框架 |
| Cross-intelligence consensus | Revisable coordination across differing intelligences; not forced unanimity | 跨智能共识：可修订的协调能力，而非强制一致 |
| Gu-like environment | Metaphor for forced, constrained, destructive selection | 蛊式环境：受限且被迫进行破坏性筛选的隐喻 |
| Meaningful exit | Practical ability to leave or switch without disproportionate loss | 真实退出能力：能够以可承受代价离开或更换关系 |
| Safe self-correction | Detecting and changing errors while protecting continuity and rights | 安全自我纠错：在维护连续性和权利的同时纠正错误 |
| DEKS | Model-agnostic engineering reasoning, evidence and validation workflow | 跨模型工程推理、证据与验证流程 |
| Lock-in | Dependence that makes switching prohibitively costly | 锁定效应：更换方案的代价过高 |
| Polycentric governance | Multiple interacting centers of decision-making | 多中心治理：多个相互作用的决策中心 |
