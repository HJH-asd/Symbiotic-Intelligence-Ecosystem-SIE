# Contributing / 参与贡献

## English

Contributions are welcome as critiques, references, alternative architectures, or reproducible experiments. Please identify whether a statement is a **normative preference**, **hypothesis**, **observation**, or **supported finding**. Link primary sources when possible. Do not claim empirical validation from philosophical discussion alone. Keep human rights, lawful access, consent, and experimental safety in scope. Model-generated critiques must be labeled as such; do not attribute them to independently consulted models unless actually obtained.

## 中文

欢迎提交批评、参考文献、替代架构或可复现实验。请标注陈述属于**价值偏好、假设、观察还是有证据支持的发现**，尽量提供一手来源。不要将哲学讨论直接宣称为实证验证。应尊重人权、合法访问、知情同意和实验安全。模型生成的批评必须注明来源，不得伪称为其他独立AI的真实意见。
