# Symbiotic Intelligence Ecosystem (SIE)

**An open, provisional research framework for cooperation, resilience, and self-correction across heterogeneous intelligences.**

> **Status:** Conceptual research proposal, not a validated architecture, deployed system, or claim of scientific priority. Version 0.5 — 2026-10-15.

SIE explores how humans, AI systems, robots, **other living organisms, natural ecosystems**, institutions, and the material infrastructures on which they depend might coexist and evolve without making survival contingent on coercive dependence, destructive competition, or unaccountable concentration of control.

The motivating question is not merely **how can different intelligences cooperate?** It is **how can an intelligent ecosystem remain capable of cooperation and correction when its members face scarcity, conflicting goals, unequal power, and pressure to preserve themselves?**

## What makes this research scope different?

SIE is **not** simply a larger multi-agent AI platform or a human–AI collaboration service. It studies **coupled human–AI–robotic–ecological systems**, including natural ecosystems as dynamic, affected systems rather than metaphors or resource inventories. It does not presume a global coordinator, universal protocol, currency, token, or score. Its contribution is a **proposed research agenda**, not a proven claim of originality or superiority. See [Scope and related concepts](docs/en/07-scope-and-distinctiveness.md).

**Key open question:** Must different intelligences transact using money or points, or can heterogeneous systems coordinate through physical accounting, reciprocity, guarantees, quotas and local agreements? See [Money and resource coordination](docs/en/08-money-and-resource-coordination.md).

## Principles of interpretation

- **Not a doctrine:** SIE may be revised, forked, or replaced by a better framework.
- **Not a single sovereign AI:** It does not propose assigning unilateral political authority to an AI.
- **Not anti-competition or anti-management:** It investigates when competition and governance enable cooperation versus entrench coercion.
- **Not proven:** Claims about resilience, self-correction, and power distribution require comparative evidence.
- **Human rights and safety matter:** Speculative AI interests must not be used to override established human rights or justify unauthorized resource acquisition.

## Relationship to DEKS

DEKS is an early engineering-knowledge and validation workflow that may serve as a small testbed for SIE ideas. DEKS is not SIE itself and should remain replaceable and model-agnostic.

## How to contribute

Critique assumptions, provide counterexamples, identify related scholarship, suggest falsifiable predictions, or propose safe, sandboxed experiments. Separate **observation**, **interpretation**, **hypothesis**, and **evidence**. Do not present simulated model outputs as statements by independent AI systems.

## 中文简介

**SIE（Symbiotic Intelligence Ecosystem，共生智能生态系统）** 是一个开放、可修订、尚待验证的研究框架，探索人类、AI、机器人、组织及其基础设施如何在资源压力、利益冲突与权力不对称下保持合作、韧性和自我纠错能力。

其研究边界包含其他生命和自然生态，不把自然系统仅作为资源或比喻；不预设所有系统使用统一货币、积分或协议。它不是单一超级AI统治方案，也不是已被证明有效的制度。其核心问题不仅是“不同智能如何合作”，更是“当生存压力与控制关系使合作难以维持时，系统如何避免陷入破坏性竞争，并能够安全地改变自身”。完整中文材料见 `docs/zh/`。
