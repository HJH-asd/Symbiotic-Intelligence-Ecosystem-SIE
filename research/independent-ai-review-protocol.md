# Independent AI critical review / 独立 AI 批判性审查协议 — v0.5

## Procedure / 流程
1. Give each reviewer the same frozen v0.5 snapshot; do not show other reviews initially. / 为每位审查者提供相同版本，先不展示其他意见。
2. Require separation of established evidence, logical derivation, speculation, and normative preferences. / 区分证据、推导、猜想与价值选择。
3. Ask for strongest counterexamples and alternative explanations; request citations only when verifiable. / 寻找最强反例与替代解释，文献必须可核实。
4. Collect independent reviews, then compare disagreement and conduct a second round. / 收集独立意见后对比分歧，再进行第二轮。
5. Preserve dissent and publish an issue-to-change log; do not optimize for consensus alone. / 保留异议并记录问题与修改，不以共识本身为目标。

## Copyable review prompt / 可复制审查提示
> Critically review SIE v0.5 as an unverified research proposal, not an established theory. Focus on the claims about cross-substrate effective information, multiscale identity, independent perspectives, ecological information impoverishment, and open-ended adaptive capacity. Identify hidden assumptions, ambiguous terms, known prior art, counterexamples (especially where centralized systems win), and operational measurements. Classify each objection as conceptual, empirical, ethical, or methodological. Propose at least three falsifiable experiments with matched resource budgets. Do not claim to have verified sources you have not accessed. Do not assume AI consciousness or rights as established. Give a revised strongest defensible thesis, and state what evidence would change your mind.
>
> 请将 SIE v0.5 作为未经验证的研究提案进行严格批判性审查，而非既定理论。重点检查跨载体有效信息、多尺度身份、独立视角、生态信息贫化与开放式适应能力。寻找隐藏前提、概念歧义、已有研究、集中式系统胜出的反例及可操作测量方法；按概念、经验、伦理和方法问题分类。提出至少三个资源预算匹配、可证伪的实验。不要声称核实了实际未访问的文献，不预设 AI 意识或权利已获证明。给出最强的可辩护修订命题，并说明何种证据会改变你的判断。
